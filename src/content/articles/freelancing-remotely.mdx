Working from Karachi for clients in other countries has been one of the best parts of my career. It also comes with habits I had to learn the hard way.

## Time zones

Overlap matters more than total hours. I agree on a fixed window each week when we are both online, and I work asynchronously the rest of the time with clear written updates.

## Contracts and payments

A short contract that states the scope, the number of revision rounds and the payment schedule prevents most misunderstandings. I ask for a deposit before starting and split the rest across milestones.

## Building trust

- Share progress early, even when it is unfinished
- Write down decisions so nobody has to remember them
- Say so quickly when something will take longer than planned

## Staying sustainable

Remote work blurs the line between work and home. I keep fixed working hours, take proper breaks and say no to projects that do not fit.

Distance is rarely the problem. Unclear communication is, and that is something every freelancer can improve.
